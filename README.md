# AiLive

### THE GALAXY HAS SOMETHING TO SAY.

**Your fleet can take a sector. Holding an empire takes people.**

*A mod for X4: Foundations, powered by Player2.*

---

Open a comm-link and put your intentions into words. The captain awaiting your orders has a career ahead of them. The station manager across the table has a business to keep alive. The council considering your offer has its own reasons to say no.

AiLive brings you into the lives and decisions behind your X4 empire through text conversations. Give a trusted officer responsibility, take an interest in the people under their command, and decide who deserves the next appointment. Between the orders and the reports, you get to know the people you keep sending into danger. An empty chair on the bridge starts to mean something.

Beyond your comms reach, you wait. A scout has information you need. A courier carries words that could end a war. Somewhere between the gates, someone else wants to know what that message says. Do you keep your eyes on the frontier, or bring the pilot home to hear the report? Who you trust with a secret matters as much as who you trust with a ship.

Your influence reaches beyond the guns of your fleet. Become the supplier a station relies on. Turn a difficult negotiation into a lasting relationship. Offer protection to a neighbour, then face the expectations that come with it. Their leaders have interests to defend, and their people have loyalties of their own. An agreement is the beginning of that relationship.

Lead from the bridge, work the trading lanes, or make your name across the negotiating table. The empire you build takes its character from the people you choose, the promises you keep, and the decisions you make when the next report arrives.

**Build an empire worth serving.**

## 📡 What's on the other end of the comm-link?

| Talk to your universe | Command your people | Follow the consequences |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Write to NPCs in your own words. Personality, mood, trust and remembered context shape the conversation. | Give captains orders and build a chain of command, from Lieutenant to Admiral. Decide who earns responsibility and who answers to whom. | Seek out intelligence, guard your secrets and follow the news as conflict and diplomacy reshape the galaxy around you. |
| Reach suitable ship and station contacts remotely, or meet someone face to face and open a text conversation. | Give an officer a mandate, delegate through the ranks and read the reports that come back. | Ship losses, sector captures and diplomatic events become part of the history people draw on when you next speak. |

Tell a captain to patrol, escort a ship or move to a new position. Read their response, follow the mission and hear when the work is done. Your officers have responsibilities within the fleet; choosing whom to promote shapes how you command it.

At the negotiating table, a manager's needs and a captain's loyalties give you different ways into a conversation. Ask questions, make your case, and listen to the answer. The person on the other end has a part in this galaxy too.
